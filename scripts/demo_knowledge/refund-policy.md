# Demo Learning Center - Official Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Standard Cancellation & Money-Back Policy

1. **Full 100% Refund (Days 1 to 7)**:
   - Students may request a full 100% refund of all tuition paid within the first 7 calendar days after the cohort start date, no questions asked.
   
2. **Pro-rated Credit (Days 8 to 21)**:
   - Between Day 8 and Day 21, students may withdraw and receive a pro-rated learning credit toward any future course or cohort valid for up to 1 year.

3. **No Refunds (After Day 21)**:
   - After Day 21 of the cohort, no monetary refunds or credits are issued due to fixed instructor and compute commitments.

## Refund Request Procedure
- To initiate a cancellation or refund, the student must notify support or their assigned advisor.
- All refund requests are reviewed by our operations team and processed within 3 to 5 business days back to the original payment method.
- Any dispute or special circumstance must be submitted to the center director for human review.
