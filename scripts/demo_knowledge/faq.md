# Demo Learning Center - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Are classes recorded if I miss a live session?
**A**: Yes! Every live lecture is recorded in high definition and uploaded to the student portal within 3 hours after class. Recordings remain accessible for 12 months after course completion.

### Q: What hardware or computer do I need?
**A**: Any modern laptop (Windows, macOS, or Linux) with at least 8GB RAM, a working webcam, and a reliable internet connection is sufficient. All required IDEs and software tools are free and open-source.

### Q: Do you offer placement or job assistance?
**A**: We provide resume reviews, technical mock interviews, and portfolio workshops. While we do not offer unconditional employment guarantees, over 85% of our graduates transition into technical roles within 6 months.

### Q: Can I switch batches if my work schedule changes?
**A**: Yes, students may request one batch transfer free of charge during the first 2 weeks of the cohort, subject to seat availability in the target batch.

### Q: Is there a certificate awarded upon graduation?
**A**: Yes. Students who maintain at least 80% class attendance and submit all milestone projects receive a verified digital certificate and LinkedIn badge.
